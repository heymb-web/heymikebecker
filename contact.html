Director and cinematographer based in Los Angeles.

I'm a documentary director and cinematographer based in Los Angeles. Over the last 13 years I've worked across five continents, directing documentary and commercial projects and shooting for major networks and brands.

My approach is built on access, trust, and patience. The best moments rarely happen on cue, so I take the time to earn my way into a story and let it surface on its own terms rather than forcing it.

That instinct shapes everything I make, on set and in the field. I hold wildland fire training that lets me work safely alongside the crews at the center of the stories I'm drawn to, close enough to capture the work as it actually happens.
